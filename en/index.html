FAQ

Parents often ask

What's the difference between kidrise.com.hk and stemtoy.com.hk?

Both are Kidrise. kidrise.com.hk is our brand site, which helps you choose by age, skill, occasion and school needs. stemtoy.com.hk is our online shop, where you order, pay and arrange delivery. The shop has an English version too.

How much is delivery?

Orders of HK$400 or more after discounts get free delivery to one address, including homes, offices, SF Express pickup points and smart lockers. Orders under HK$400 have a HK$60 delivery and handling fee. Some remote areas have a surcharge.

How long does delivery take? What if I need a gift urgently?

Orders placed before 4pm Monday to Friday are usually delivered in 5–7 working days. If it's urgent, WhatsApp us to check stock first. Some items can be collected in Kwun Tong (Monday to Friday, pay before pickup).

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes. Add gift wrapping at checkout in the online shop.

Do you have guides to choosing toys?

Yes. Our parenting articles cover telescopes, microscopes, STEM toys and toys for every age, and you can print the free toy buying checklist for ages 1–12.

Can schools get samples first?

Yes. Fill in your class size, year level and theme on our Schools page and we'll arrange samples and a formal quotation.
