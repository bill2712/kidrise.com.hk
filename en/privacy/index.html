Privacy policy

Kidrise 童樂高飛 (“we”) cares about your and your child's privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal data when you use kidrise.com.hk (“this website”), in line with Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486). If there is any difference between the English and Chinese versions, the Chinese version prevails.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers this website. If you shop, create an account or pay at the Kidrise online shop, stemtoy.com.hk, that information is handled under the shop's privacy policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

The forms on this website do not store any data on a server or in a database. When you press a form button, the website only opens WhatsApp or your email app on your own device with a pre-filled message. We receive the following only after you choose to send it:

  • Explorer Kit waitlist: your name, your child's age range, the kits you're interested in, any comments, and your email address if you provide it. We do not ask for children's names or photos.
  • School and organisation quote requests: organisation name, contact person's name, role, email, phone and enquiry details.
  • Activity updates: if you message us on WhatsApp to receive updates on new activities and free resources, we receive your phone number.
  • Other WhatsApp or email enquiries: your phone number or email address and your message.

Information collected automatically

  • Website analytics: we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the website is used, such as pages viewed, device and browser, approximate location (city level), referring website, and button clicks (for example, going to the shop or printing an activity card). This is analysed statistically and we do not use it to identify individuals.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to enquiries, provide quotations and samples, and follow up on orders.
  • With your consent, to tell you by WhatsApp or email about new activities, free resources and Explorer Kit launches.
  • To analyse and improve the website's content and experience.
  • To meet legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Service providers and transfers

To provide these services we use the following providers, whose servers may be outside Hong Kong:

  • Google Analytics: website analytics.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only when you choose to contact us or register by WhatsApp.
  • Email service provider: when you contact us or register by email, messages are processed and stored by our email service.
  • GitHub Pages: this website's hosting service, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

5. Direct marketing

We only send promotional messages by WhatsApp or email after you actively register or ask for updates and agree to receive them. These may cover family activities, free resources, Kidrise products and Explorer Kit news. You can reply “stop” at any time, or email contact@kidrise.com.hk, to ask us to stop using your data for direct marketing, free of charge.

6. Cookies and Google Analytics

Google Analytics uses cookies to analyse how the website is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Turning off cookies won't affect browsing this website or printing activity cards.

7. Data retention

  • Activity update and Explorer Kit waitlist details: kept until you opt out or ask us to delete them.
  • Enquiry and quotation details: kept as needed for business and legal purposes after the enquiry or transaction is complete, then deleted.
  • Website analytics data: deleted automatically according to the retention period set in Google Analytics.

8. Your rights

Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, you have the right to access and correct the personal data we hold about you, and to ask us to stop using it for direct marketing. Email contact@kidrise.com.hk and we will reply within a reasonable time. We may charge a reasonable fee for handling a data access request, as allowed by the Ordinance.

9. Children's privacy

This website is for parents, carers and teachers. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you find that your child has given us personal data without your consent, please contact us and we will delete it as soon as possible.

10. Security

This website uses HTTPS encryption throughout. Only staff who need to handle enquiries can access your data, and we take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorised access, processing or loss.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with the date it was updated.

12. Contact us

For any questions about this policy or your personal data, contact:
Email: contact@kidrise.com.hk
WhatsApp: 6672 1884
