🔭 Make a Kaleidoscope: Template and Pattern Log

Ages 6–10Craft & observe1 page, A4

Free printable: a kaleidoscope template with a fold-up triangular tube, pattern discs and a log to draw what you see. Use mirrors or foil on card to learn about reflection and symmetry.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it (household version)

  1. Print the template on card and cut out the tube template along the solid lines.
  2. Stick foil, as smooth as possible, on the shiny side of the template, or use the safety mirrors from the kit.
  3. Fold along the dotted lines into a triangular tube, foil facing in, and tape it closed.
  4. Cut out a pattern disc, colour it in and tape it to one end of the tube.
  5. Look through the other end towards the light and slowly turn the disc to see the repeating patterns.

The science

  • Three mirror surfaces reflect each other, turning one pattern into many.
  • Kaleidoscope patterns are symmetrical, with several lines of symmetry.
  • The smoother the foil, the clearer the reflection. That's why mirrors are made so flat.

Safety: use scissors and mirrors with an adult, and never point the kaleidoscope at the sun.
