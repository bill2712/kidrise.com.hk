🛂 Nature Explorer Mission Passport

Ages 4–9Observation log1 page, A4

Free printable: the Nature Explorer Kit mission passport to record the date, place and biggest discovery for 5 nature missions. Collect 5 stamps to become a Nature Explorer.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Before the first mission, write your name in the passport and add a photo or self-portrait.
  2. After each mission, fill in the date and place and write or draw your biggest discovery.
  3. A parent stamps, signs or adds a sticker in the stamp box.
  4. Collect all 5 stamps, sign the certificate and become a Nature Explorer!

Tips for parents

  • Missions don't have to be done in order. Swap them around for the weather and your schedule.
  • The “biggest discovery” can be a leaf, a sound or a question. There's no right or wrong.
  • When it's finished, keep the passport in a folder with the observation records.
🍃 Explorer Kit mission sheet: this worksheet goes with the Nature Explorer Kit, but you can use it without the kit, using things you have at home. See the kit page for every mission step and parent question.
