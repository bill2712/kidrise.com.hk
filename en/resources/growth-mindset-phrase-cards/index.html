🌱 Growth Mindset Phrase Cards (12 phrases)

Ages 4–12Feelings & character1 page, A4 (cut into 12 cards)

Free printable: 12 growth mindset phrase cards that turn “I can't do it” into “I can't do it yet”, to stick on a desk or fridge and help children face challenges and keep trying.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Print and cut into 12 cards. Laminate them or stick them onto card.
  2. Put them on the desk, the fridge or in the school bag, and read one when things get hard.
  3. Choose a “phrase of the week” and share at dinner when you used it.

How parents can help

  • When you hear your child say the top phrase, gently help them swap it for the bottom one.
  • Use these phrases about your own challenges too, like “I don't know how to use this app yet, I'll try again.”
  • Praise effort, strategies and progress, not just results.

KIDRISE · GROWTH MINDSET

🌱 Swap the Sentence Cards

Cut along the lines. When you want to say the top line, try the bottom one instead!

“I can't do it.”→ I can't do it yet, and I can learn!
“It's too hard!”→ Hard means I'm learning something new.
“I give up.”→ I'll try another way.
“I made a mistake.”→ Mistakes show me what to improve.
“She's better than me.”→ I can ask how she did it.
“It's good enough.”→ Is this my best work?
“I'll never be able to.”→ With practice, I'll get better.
“I'm not smart enough.”→ My brain grows stronger with practice.
“I don't want to try.”→ I won't know until I try!
“I'm scared of getting it wrong.”→ Scientists try many times before they succeed.
“I don't get it.”→ I can ask a question.
“Finished!”→ What did I learn today?
