🐞 Little Bug Hunt Log

Ages 4–10Outdoor game1 page, A4

Free printable: a bug hunt log with 9 common creatures to find and a drawing box to record where, how many and what they were doing. For parks and walks, building observation and respect for life.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Take the sheet, a pencil and a magnifier to a park, flower bed or country park.
  2. When you find a creature on the sheet, note where it was and how many you saw.
  3. Pick the most interesting one, draw it below and watch what it's doing.

Bug hunt rules

  • Look only: don't catch or touch, and keep a step away.
  • If you turn over a stone, put it back. It's someone's home.
  • If you meet bees, wasps or caterpillars, move away slowly and don't swat them.

Tip: snails and earthworms come out more in the early morning or after rain; sunny flower beds are best for butterflies and bees.
