🌙 30-Day Moon Phase Log

Ages 5–10Observation log1 page, A4

Free printable: the Kidrise 30-day moon phase log, where children aged 5–10 draw the moon every night and discover the pattern of its phases for themselves. With tips for parents.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Stick it on the fridge or by a window and look for the moon at about the same time each night.
  2. Draw the moon's shape in the circle. If you can't see it, draw ✕ and circle the weather.
  3. Look back together each week: is the moon getting bigger or smaller?
  4. After 30 days, ask your child to explain the moon's pattern in their own words.

What children learn

  • The patience to observe and record over time, a scientist's most important habit.
  • The moon's cycle takes about 29.5 days: new moon → first quarter → full moon → last quarter.
  • Moonlight is actually reflected sunlight.

Tip: around the first quarter, the moon is already up in the early evening, perfect for children. With a telescope, the craters along the line between light and dark are especially clear.
