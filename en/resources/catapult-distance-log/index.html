🏹 Catapult Distance Log

Ages 6–10Worksheet1 page, A4

Free printable: a catapult distance log for testing how the pivot position changes how far a lolly stick catapult launches, 3 tests per position, to learn about levers and comparing data.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Build a lolly stick and rubber band catapult following the activity card.
  2. Stick a start line on the floor and put the catapult behind it.
  3. Put the pivot at position A (near your hand), launch a paper ball 3 times and measure each distance.
  4. Move the pivot to position B (middle) and C (near the launch cup) and repeat.
  5. Work out the average distance for each position to find the best design.

Understanding levers

  • Pivot (fulcrum): the point the lever turns on.
  • Effort: where you press down.
  • Load: where the launch cup and paper ball sit.
  • Moving the pivot changes the launch force and distance. A seesaw works the same way.

Safety: only launch paper balls or cotton wool balls, and never aim at people or animals.
