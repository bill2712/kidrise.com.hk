⛅ 7-Day Weather Chart

Ages 4–9Observation log1 page, A4

Free printable: a 7-day weather chart to record the weather, temperature, clouds and what to wear, helping children aged 4–9 build a daily habit of observing.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Each morning before you go out, look out of the window together and circle the weather.
  2. Older children can check the Hong Kong Observatory forecast and record the highest and lowest temperatures.
  3. Let your child decide from the weather: “Do I need an umbrella?” “Long or short sleeves?”
  4. After a week, compare: which day was hottest? How many days did it rain?

What children learn

  • Weather symbols and temperatures, linking science to everyday decisions.
  • Comparing and summarising a week of data.
