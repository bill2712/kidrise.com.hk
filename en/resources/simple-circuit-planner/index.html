💡 Simple Circuit Planner and Conductor Test

Ages 7–11Worksheet1 page, A4

Free printable: a simple circuit planner to learn the symbols for a battery, wire, bulb and switch, draw a closed circuit, and test whether 10 household items are conductors or insulators.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Learn the four circuit symbols: battery, wire, bulb (or LED) and switch.
  2. In the box, draw a closed circuit that would light the bulb.
  3. Connect a battery box, crocodile clip wires and an LED to test your design.
  4. Leave a gap in the circuit and put household items in it one by one, recording whether the LED lights.

Safety rules

  • Only use 1.5V AA or AAA batteries, and never connect to a mains socket.
  • Don't open batteries. If a battery gets hot, stop at once and tell an adult.
  • LEDs have a + and − side. Connected the wrong way they won't light, but they won't break either. Just swap them round.
