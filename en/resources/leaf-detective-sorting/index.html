🍂 Leaf Detective Sorting Sheet

Ages 4–9Observation log1 page, A4

Free printable: a leaf detective sorting sheet that guides children to observe fallen leaves by shape, edge, size and colour, then sort them by their own rules, building observation and classification skills.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Collect 6 fallen leaves of different shapes in the park.
  2. Number each leaf and circle its features on the sheet: shape, edge, size and colour.
  3. Sort the leaves into two groups using your own rule, and write the rule down.
  4. Challenge: find a second way to sort the same pile.

Leaf features to look for

  • Shape: round, heart, needle, hand-shaped, or feather-like (made of many small leaflets).
  • Edge: smooth, toothed or wavy.
  • Veins: branching from the middle like a feather, or spreading from one point like a hand.

When scientists sort things, the most important thing is a clear rule: anyone following your rule should get the same groups.
