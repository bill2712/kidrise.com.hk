✅ Toy Buying Checklist for Ages 1–12

For parents (ages 1–12)Buying guide1 page, A4

Free printable: a toy buying checklist for ages 1–12 listing what children develop at each age, recommended toy types and safety points, for buying gifts or toys for your own child.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

Before buying a toy or a birthday gift, find the child's age, see which skills matter most at that stage, then tick the toy types that fit. The checklist also shows safety points for each age so you don't buy the wrong thing.

Three key rules

  • The right age matters more than “advanced”: toys that are too hard only frustrate.
  • More than one way to play: open-ended toys stay with children for longer.
  • A grown-up plays too: playing together is what makes a toy worthwhile.
