🔺 Toddler Colour and Shape Hunt

Ages 2–4Early learning1 page, A4

Free printable: a toddler colour and shape hunt to learn 5 colours and 5 shapes, for children aged 2–4 to use at home or out and about.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Colour in each dot with the matching colour first (or let your child do it).
  2. When you find something of the same colour or shape at home or out, add a sticker or a tick.
  3. Say the names as you go: “A red car!” “A round clock!”

Early learning focus

  • Recognising colours and shapes is an important milestone at ages 2–4.
  • Learning in real surroundings sticks better than looking at flashcards.
  • Lots of conversation builds your child's vocabulary at the same time.
