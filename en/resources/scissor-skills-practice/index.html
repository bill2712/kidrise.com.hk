✂️ Scissor Skills Practice (straight, zigzag, wave, spiral)

Ages 3–6Fine motor practice1 page, A4

Free printable: 4 levels of scissor practice, from straight lines to zigzags, waves and spirals, gradually building fine motor skills, hand–eye coordination and two-handed control for ages 3–6.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Use round-tipped children's scissors with an adult nearby.
  2. Start with level 1 straight lines, then move on to zigzags, waves and spirals.
  3. Remind your child “thumbs up”: the thumb of the cutting hand on top, while the other hand turns the paper.
  4. 5–10 minutes at a time is plenty. Rest when hands get tired.

What children learn

  • Controlling the force of opening and closing their fingers (fine motor skills).
  • Using two hands for different jobs: one cuts, one holds and turns the paper.
  • Hand–eye coordination and focus, getting ready for holding a pencil and writing.

Tip: print on thicker paper for easier control, and stick the cut strips into a collage afterwards.
