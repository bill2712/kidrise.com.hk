🚗 Ramp Car Race Log (slopes and friction)

Ages 6–10Worksheet1 page, A4

Free printable: a ramp car race log testing how ramp height and floor surface change how far a toy car rolls, to learn fair testing, measuring and friction.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Build a ramp from stiff card and books. Put the car at the top (same spot each time) and let go without pushing.
  2. Experiment 1: with the ramp on 1, 2 and 3 books, test 3 times each and measure how far the car rolls from the bottom.
  3. Experiment 2: keep it at 2 books and cover the floor at the bottom with different materials (floor, towel, foil, carpet), then test again.
  4. Work out the averages and look for a pattern.

Understanding friction

  • Friction is the force that slows things down when two surfaces rub together.
  • Rough surfaces (like a towel) have more friction, so the car stops sooner.
  • The higher the ramp, the faster the car goes, but too high and it may tip over!
