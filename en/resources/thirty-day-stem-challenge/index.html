🚀 30-Day STEM Challenge Chart

Ages 4–12Holiday plan1 page, A4

Free printable: a 30-day STEM challenge chart covering light, forces, living things and materials, 15–30 minutes a day, perfect for the summer, Christmas and Easter holidays.

Preview the worksheet

How to use it

  1. Print it before the holiday starts and put it somewhere everyone can see.
  2. Do one challenge a day and colour in its box when you finish.
  3. Do them in any order: indoor challenges on rainy days, outdoor ones when it's sunny.
  4. Set small rewards at 10, 20 and 30 boxes, like choosing the weekend activity.

Four themes

  • ☀️ Light and shadow: shadows, colour, reflection
  • 🌉 Forces and structures: towers, bridges, launching
  • 🌱 Living things and nature: plants, insects, weather
  • 🧪 Materials and change: dissolving, floating, chemical reactions

Use it with the holiday STEM plan for detailed weekly themes. Most challenges have a full activity card in the activity library.
