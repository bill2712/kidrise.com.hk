Why a kit?

Buying a toy vs. buying an experience

Kidrise Explorer Kit

Open it and play

  • Mission cards a parent can read in 3 minutes, with full English instructions on this site
  • 5 missions that build step by step, for 5 weekends of play
  • All the special tools and materials included

What's inside

In every box

Based on the first kit, Nature Explorer. Final contents may change at launch.

Explorer tools

A magnifier, bug viewer, ruler and collection bags: real tools you can use again and again.

Mission materials

Seeds, rubbing paper, specimen cards and more, packed separately for each mission.

Mission cards and journal

Illustrated cards in Traditional Chinese with parent question prompts and record pages. Every mission, and all the mission worksheets, are available in English on this site.

QR code videos

A 2–3 minute demo for every mission that children can follow on their own.

Nature Explorer:
5 missions from home to the park

Each mission takes about 20–40 minutes. Finish them all to earn the “Nature Explorer” sticker in your journal.

Ages 4–9Indoors + outdoorsWith an adult
See every step and parent question →
  1. Leaf DetectiveCollect fallen leaves, sort them by shape and colour, and rub the veins
  2. Tiny UniverseLook closely at soil, petals and bugs and draw a first observation report
  3. Seed DiaryRecord a seed's changes for 7 days and learn what plants need
  4. Sound MapClose your eyes, listen, and draw the sounds as a map

Kits FAQ

Explorer Kit questions

When will the kits launch?

The first kit, Nature Explorer, is in small-batch production and family testing. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you first before launch.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. Kits will be sold individually at first, with no monthly fee and no automatic charges. If we add a subscription later, it will be optional.

Are the mission cards in English?

The printed cards are in Traditional Chinese. Every mission has full English instructions, parent questions and extension ideas on this site, plus English mission worksheets to print, so English-speaking families can follow along easily.

Do parents need a science background?

Not at all. Every mission has a mission card, parent question prompts and a demo video, so you can get started in 3 minutes.

Can schools or learning centres buy in bulk?

Yes. We also make Classroom Discovery Kits for classes of 20–30. Ask us for a quote or samples.
