What shall we do today?

Buying for someone else's child, the worry is always that they already have it or it's the wrong age. Educational toys are a safe choice: parents like them and children play with them for longer. Here's a guide by age and budget to help you pick a thoughtful gift in five minutes.

Prices below are shop prices in November 2026. Final prices and stock are confirmed at the stemtoy.com.hk checkout.

3 questions to ask before you buy

  1. How old is the child? Go by their actual age, and watch for small parts with under-3s.
  2. Do they like quiet or active play? Observers enjoy science toys; hands-on kids enjoy models and building; social butterflies enjoy board games.
  3. Do they have space at home? Hong Kong homes are small, so compact gifts that store easily are popular with parents.

Ideas by budget

Under $199: party gifts

Great for classmates' parties and gift swaps. STEM wooden models (about $89–109) and moving paper models (about $119) are small and can be built on the spot, and primary school kids love them.

$200–399: the most popular birthday gifts

Family board games, magnetic bead mazes, colour code games and kids' binoculars: the whole family can play, again and again.

$400–699: a substantial gift

A kids' telescope, an explorer microscope, a maths strategy game or a hydraulic kit: big gifts that stay with a child for years.

$700+: something special

A digital microscope or a telescope and microscope bundle, ideal for graduation, starting P1 or Christmas.

4 ways to make a gift more thoughtful

  • Add an activity sheet: give a telescope with a moon phase log, or a microscope with an experiment log.
  • Write an invitation to play: for example, “Next time we meet, let's look at the moon together!”
  • Gift wrapping: add wrapping when you order in the online shop.
  • Allow time for delivery: orders usually arrive in 5–7 working days. For urgent gifts, WhatsApp us to check stock or arrange pickup in Kwun Tong.

FAQ

What if they already have the same toy?

DIY models, consumable activity kits and board games are less likely to be duplicates. Or simply ask their parents what they're into at the moment.

Can you send it straight to the birthday child?

Yes. Enter the recipient's address at checkout. Each order can go to one address.

I need a dozen small gifts for a party. Are there discounts?

For bulk orders, WhatsApp us. Schools and organisations can also request a quote on our Schools page.
