Looking for more family activity ideas?

What shall we do today?

Christmas gifts tend to end one of two ways: played with for a day and then put away, or duplicated by another relative. A gift that lasts, teaches something and can be played as a family means more than chasing the latest trend.

3 rules for gifting this year: ① match your child's current interests, not what adults think they “should” learn; ② choose toys that can be played again and grow with them; ③ allow time for delivery: couriers are busier from mid-December, so order one to two weeks early.

Christmas gifts by budget

🎁 Under $199: gift swaps

Wooden models, paper models and torch projectors: small, surprising and ideal for school Christmas parties or swapping with cousins.

🎁 $200–399: the main gift

Board games, mazes, maths puzzles and binoculars the whole family can enjoy over the holidays.

🎁 $400–699: a big, thoughtful gift

Large magnetic tile sets, star projectors and play kitchens: “hero toys” that get played with for years.

🎁 $700+: something special

A telescope and microscope bundle or a digital microscope, for children with a real passion for science.

Tips to make a gift last

  • Add a “mission card”: with a telescope, for example, write “Let's find the moon on Christmas Eve” so the gift comes with its first activity.
  • Don't give everything at once: if there are several gifts, spread them between Christmas and New Year, or use a toy rotation planner.
  • Play the first time together: if the first go is fun, children will come back to it on their own.

Christmas countdown: 24 days of family STEM

The wait before Christmas can be fun too. Our Christmas STEM advent calendar has a 10-minute mission for every day from 1 December, like making paper snowflakes, testing how fast ice melts and building the tallest Christmas tree tower, all with things you have at home.

FAQ

When should I order Christmas gifts?

We suggest ordering before early December. Couriers are busy in the week before Christmas. If you need it by a certain date, choose pickup or ask us early.

Can I add gift wrapping?

Yes, you can add gift wrapping at checkout in the online shop. See the shop page for details.

I don't know what toys they already have. How do I avoid duplicates?

Choose consumable or expandable gifts, such as science experiment materials, extra magnetic tiles or a new board game, or simply ask the parents what their child is into right now.
