🏗️ One-Minute Tower

Ages 4–9 | About 15 min | Indoors | Building

🧰 You'll need

  • Paper cups, blocks or index cards
  • A timer
  • A tape measure

🧭 How to play

  1. Set the rules: build the tallest tower in one minute, and it must stand for 5 seconds.
  2. Round one: build freely, then measure the height.
  3. Talk about it: would a wider base be steadier?
  4. Round two: improve the design and compare the two heights.
🎓 What they learn: The engineering design cycle of design, test and improve, and how a wider base makes a structure more stable.

💬 Questions to ask

  • Why did the tower fall?
  • Which shape is the steadiest?
  • If you had two minutes, what would you change?

🚀 Extension challenge

Add a typhoon test: blow a fan at the tower for 5 seconds and see if it stays up.

Note: Always do activities with an adult.
