✈️ Paper Plane Lab

Ages 5–10 | About 30 min | Indoors | Building

🧰 You'll need

  • A few sheets of A4 paper
  • Paper clips
  • A tape measure (or count steps)
  • Paper and pencil to record

🧭 How to play

  1. Everyone folds a plane, flies it three times and records the distances.
  2. Clip a paper clip to the nose and fly it three more times.
  3. Change the size or shape of the wings and test again.
  4. Compare the data and choose the best design.
🎓 What they learn: Practises fair testing and recording data, with a first look at lift and centre of gravity.

💬 Questions to ask

  • With the paper clip, did it fly further or shorter?
  • Why fly each design three times?
  • What changes if you fold the wingtips up a little?

🚀 Extension challenge

Hold a family flying contest with two prizes: furthest and most accurate.

⚠️ Safety: Don't throw paper planes at people's faces. Always do activities with an adult.
