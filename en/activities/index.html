For parents

Family activities:
parents only need to do three things

You don't need to be a scientist. What matters most is being curious together.

1. Ask “What do you think will happen?”

Let your child predict before you start. Wrong guesses are fine: comparing the prediction with the result is where scientific thinking begins.

2. Let them do it

You take care of safety and hand over everything else, including pouring, measuring and tidying up.

3. Record it together

Draw it, photograph it or explain it to another family member. They'll remember it for longer.

⚠️ Safety: all activities should be done with an adult. Keep small objects away from children under 3. An adult should handle scissors, hot water and household chemicals such as vinegar and baking soda.
