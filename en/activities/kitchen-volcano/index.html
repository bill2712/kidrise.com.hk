🌋 Kitchen Volcano

Ages 4–9 | About 15 min | Indoors | Science

🧰 You'll need

  • 2 tablespoons baking soda
  • Half a cup of white vinegar
  • A little washing-up liquid and red food colouring
  • A plastic cup and a tray

🧭 How to play

  1. Put the cup in the middle of the tray and add the baking soda.
  2. Add a little washing-up liquid and colouring.
  3. Pour in the vinegar and watch the eruption.
  4. Change the amounts. Do you get more bubbles?
🎓 What they learn: An acid and a base react to make a gas (carbon dioxide).

💬 Questions to ask

  • What's inside the bubbles?
  • Will more vinegar make it erupt for longer?
  • Why add washing-up liquid?

🚀 Extension challenge

Put baking soda in a balloon, stretch it over a bottle of vinegar, tip it in and see if the gas inflates the balloon.

⚠️ Safety: Keep vinegar away from eyes, and wash hands afterwards. Always do activities with an adult.
