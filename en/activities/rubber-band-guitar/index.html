🎸 Rubber Band Guitar

Ages 4–8 | About 20 min | Indoors | Science

🧰 You'll need

  • A tissue box or open box
  • 4–5 rubber bands of different thicknesses
  • 2 pencils

🧭 How to play

  1. Stretch the rubber bands over the box, from thick to thin.
  2. Slide a pencil under the bands at each end to lift them off the box.
  3. Pluck each band and compare the sounds.
  4. Tighten or loosen a band. What do you hear?
🎓 What they learn: Sound comes from vibration, and how fast something vibrates sets the pitch.

💬 Questions to ask

  • Which sounds higher, a thin band or a thick one?
  • Can you see the band vibrate when you pluck it?
  • Can you play a simple song?

🚀 Extension challenge

Cut a round hole in the box and compare how loud it sounds with and without the hole.

⚠️ Safety: Don't stretch rubber bands towards anyone's eyes. Always do activities with an adult.
