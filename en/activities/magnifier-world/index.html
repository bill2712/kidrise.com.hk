🔬 The World Under a Magnifier

Ages 5–10 | About 20 min | Indoors | Science

🧰 You'll need

  • A magnifying glass or kids' microscope
  • Salt, sugar, hair, fabric, a leaf
  • White paper and coloured pens

🧭 How to play

  1. First look at each sample with the naked eye and draw it.
  2. Then look again with the magnifier or microscope.
  3. Compare what's new, for example that salt grains are cube-shaped.
  4. Draw or photograph the most interesting discovery as an observation report.
🎓 What they learn: Introduces magnifying tools and builds the habit of careful observation and recording.

💬 Questions to ask

  • How do salt and sugar look different up close?
  • Is your hair the same as Mum's?
  • Why does fabric have little squares?

🚀 Extension challenge

Look at onion skin under a microscope to see what plant cells look like.

⚠️ Safety: Take care with microscope slides. An adult should handle any broken glass. Always do activities with an adult.
