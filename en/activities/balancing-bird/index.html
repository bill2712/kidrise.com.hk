🐦 Balancing Bird

Ages 6–10 | About 25 min | Indoors | Building

🧰 You'll need

  • Cardboard
  • 2 one-dollar coins
  • Tape, scissors and coloured pens

🧭 How to play

  1. Draw a bird with outstretched wings on cardboard and cut it out.
  2. Tape a coin under each wingtip.
  3. Rest the beak on your fingertip and see if it balances.
  4. Move the coins to find the steadiest balance point.
🎓 What they learn: Introduces centre of gravity and balance, key ideas in mechanical and structural design.

💬 Questions to ask

  • Can the bird balance without the coins?
  • What happens if you move the coins forward or back?
  • What else stays up by balancing?

🚀 Extension challenge

Try balancing the bird on a pen top, a bottle rim or a string.

⚠️ Safety: An adult should help cut the cardboard. Always do activities with an adult.
