🎲 Story Dice

Ages 4–8 | About 20 min | Indoors | Pretend play

🧰 You'll need

  • 3 dice (or paper slips to draw)
  • Paper and pencil to draw 18 pictures (6 characters, 6 places, 6 events)

🧭 How to play

  1. Together, list 6 characters, 6 places and 6 events, numbered 1–6.
  2. Roll three dice to get today's story combination.
  3. One person says the first sentence and everyone takes turns adding the next.
  4. When the story ends, give it a title and draw a cover.
🎓 What they learn: Develops language, sequencing and creativity, plus listening and taking turns.

💬 Questions to ask

  • What was the most exciting part of the story?
  • If you were the main character, what would you do?
  • What's the best way for the story to end?

🚀 Extension challenge

Record the story, or write it up as a 4-page mini book.

Note: Always do activities with an adult.
