Gift FAQ

Gift questions

How early should I order for a birthday?

Orders placed before 4pm Monday to Friday are usually delivered in 5–7 working days, so we suggest ordering at least 7 working days ahead. If time is tight, WhatsApp us to check stock first, or choose items from our Kwun Tong pickup range.

Can you send it straight to the birthday child?

Yes. Each order has one delivery address, so enter the recipient's address at checkout. For different addresses, please place separate orders.

What if they already have the same toy?

Family games, DIY models and consumable activity kits are less likely to be duplicates. If there's a problem after delivery, contact us. Arrangements follow the shop's after-sales terms.

Can prices change?

Prices on this page are for reference. Final prices, offers and stock are confirmed at the stemtoy.com.hk checkout.
