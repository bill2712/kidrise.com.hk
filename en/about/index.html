About Kidrise

From a STEM toy shop
to growing with your child.

Kidrise 童樂高飛 was founded in Hong Kong in 2020. We believe children learn best through play: doing, asking, failing and trying again. We started as an online STEM toy shop, choosing learning tools for families and schools and developing our own products. Today our promise is Play. Learn. Grow., for even more Hong Kong families.

Vision

To be the children's play and learning brand Hong Kong parents trust most, keeping every child curious.

Mission

To inspire with hand-picked toys, build family experiences with our Explorer Kits, support schools with classroom kits, and help families with free activities.

Approach

We blend play and learning, combine STEM with the spirit of Montessori education, and care about the whole child, quality and safety.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are Kidrise and stemtoy.com.hk the same company?

Yes. stemtoy.com.hk is Kidrise's online shop and STEM specialist store, and kidrise.com.hk is our brand site. Kidrise handles all orders, payments and after-sales service.

Why are your prices sometimes different from other shops?

We regularly review prices and costs to keep them fair. We also put a lot into choosing products, age guidance, play notes and after-sales support. If you spot a price difference, let us know on WhatsApp.

How do you decide the age range?

We start from the manufacturer's age labels and safety warnings, then group toys by developmental stage and how hard they are to play. Age ranges are a guide. Take extra care with small parts for children under 3.

How can I pay?

The shop accepts a range of online payment methods, shown at the stemtoy.com.hk checkout. Schools and organisations can pay against a quotation.

What if there's a problem with my order?

Keep the packaging and contact us as soon as possible. Arrangements follow the shop's returns policy.

Do you speak English?

Yes. You're welcome to WhatsApp or email us in English or Chinese.

Questions?
We're happy to help you choose.

Not sure which to buy? Tell us your child's age and interests and we'll give you personal suggestions.
