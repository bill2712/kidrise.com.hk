From the 2025/26 school year, the Education Bureau is introducing Primary Science as a standalone subject, starting in P1 and P4 and extending to every year level over the following years. Schools increasingly need hands-on materials that are ready to use and reusable.

Schools FAQ

School questions

Can we try a sample first?

Yes. Choose “Quote and sample” in the enquiry form and we'll confirm the sample arrangements with you.

Can the sets be adapted to our curriculum?

Classroom Discovery Kits are standard sets, which keeps quality consistent and delivery fast. Student numbers, class numbers and some activity content can be adjusted for your school.

Are materials available in English?

Teacher's guides and student sheets are currently written in Traditional Chinese. If you teach in English, tell us in the enquiry form and we'll discuss what we can provide for your class.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on quantity and stock. The formal quotation shows the expected delivery date. For big events or the start of term, please contact us early.

Can you provide quotations and invoices?

Yes. We provide formal quotations and invoices to fit school procurement and funding claim procedures.

We only need a few items. Is that OK?

Yes. Order directly from the primary STEM range in our shop, or contact us about bulk prices.
